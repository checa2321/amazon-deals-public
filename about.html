How this works

One rule: 50% off or it doesn't make the page.

Why this exists

My name is Checarlos. This whole thing started because, I don't know about you, but I'm tired of buying something and then realizing afterwards that I paid the "expensive" price for it. At some point I got one of those deals apps, and it was useful, but I always felt like it wasn't really giving me the best of the best. So I decided to build my own system, where I know it's the best offer available because my own system is the one finding it, and I don't have to depend on anybody else.

What counts as a deal here

Everything on this page is at least 50% off its listed price. That's not a guideline, it's a filter. Anything under 50% never makes it onto the page at all.

You're asking why 50%? Because if I'm already spending the time to look for a product, I'd rather it be something where I buy it and go "wow, that actually came out cheap," instead of something where I saved two or three dollars. I want the amount to be big enough that the purchase is worth being happy about, not one where I end up paying like I'd walked into the store and paid full price or more. We all know the big retailers overprice material things by a lot, and finding the best deal is the whole priority of this service.

Where the listings come from

  • eBay, through eBay's official Browse API. Honestly, a lot of things here are cheaper than they are on Amazon.
  • Walmart, from Walmart's public deals listings.
  • Slickdeals, DealNews and r/deals, community and editorial deal feeds, used as leads. I include them because, hey, whatever my system finds is worth showing. Even if I don't personally see the point of something, somebody else might think it's fantastic, and the more there is, the better.

How often it updates, and what "seen" means

The page rebuilds every hour. Each listing shows how long ago it was last confirmed in a feed.

A deal stays on the page until it hasn't shown up in any source for three days. At that point it gets dropped. Not because I know it's dead, but because I can't vouch for it anymore.

My best advice: once you find a deal, if you're not buying it right away, at least put it on a list of your own or save it as a bookmark. That way you still have it even if it disappears from my page. And keep in mind that the whole point of this page is to find you the best deal, and at 50% off we both know an item like that isn't going to last long. So take advantage of it!

What this does not do

  • Prices change at the retailer. What's here is what it was the last time it was checked, not a guarantee.
  • No stock guarantee. An item can be listed here and be sold out by the time you click. Another thing: if we're talking about clothing, it might not be there in your size. That's happened to me personally.
  • No price history and no analysis of whether something is actually a good price historically. This is a discount filter, not a research tool. For that there are other tools worth recommending, like Keepa.
  • Nothing here is hand-tested. I haven't used these products and I'm not reviewing them.
  • If you're outside the US, some items may not ship to you, especially on eBay. That's happened to me with orders going to Puerto Rico, and then the deal is useless to you. :(
